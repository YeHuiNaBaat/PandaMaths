Class X - Chapter 8: Introduction to Trigonometry

Trigonometry is the study of relationships between the sides and angles of a triangle[cite: 2].

sine of ?A (sin A): Side opposite to angle A / Hypotenuse[cite: 2]

cosine of ?A (cos A): Side adjacent to angle A / Hypotenuse[cite: 2]

tangent of ?A (tan A): sin A / cos A[cite: 2]

cotangent of ?A (cot A): 1 / tan A[cite: 2]

secant of ?A (sec A): 1 / cos A[cite: 2]

cosecant of ?A (cosec A): 1 / sin A[cite: 2]
